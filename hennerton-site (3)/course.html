The Course

Eighteen holes above the Thames Valley

A par-65 parkland course of 4,391 yards that prizes accuracy over length — with dramatic elevation, a fine set of par threes, and views that stretch from Reading to Henley.

The layout

Strategy, not just distance

Hennerton opened as a nine-hole course in May 1992 — inaugurated by former Ryder Cup captain Bernard Gallacher, with the original holes designed by Colonel Dion Beard. In June 2006 the club extended to a full eighteen, the new loop shaped by architects Gaunt & Marnoch. The result is a course of terraced tees, contoured greens and constant changes in elevation that asks you to think your way round.

Set on free-draining chalk, it stays playable through the seasons, and an automatic irrigation system keeps it in condition year-round.

Course data

18 holes · Par 65 · 4,391 yards (back tees) · Course rating 63.2 · Slope 106 · Parkland · Free-draining chalk · Buggies & club hire available.

Holes to remember

The pick of the round

16th — the signature

A 183-yard par 3 played across a sharp valley, with the whole countryside opening up behind the flag. The most photographed hole on the course.

The par threes

Hennerton is known for a strong, varied collection of one-shotters that can make or break a card.

The elevation

Terraced tees and rolling ground give you a different look — and a different view — on almost every hole.
